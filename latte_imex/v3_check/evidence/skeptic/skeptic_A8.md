## 3. Attempts to refute

1. *"The κ_r V_R radial return flow is a cup-surface flow."* No — it is a target velocity applied only where s+Λ > 0, i.e. within the 8×5 mm footprint (L333–350; `stepB_prepare` uses `f = dt(s+Λ)`). Outside K the film feels only pressure, viscosity and −βq.
2. *"The §14 delay patch gives the model a submerged liquid-milk phase."* The reservoir `msub` is foam-to-be (it surfaces entirely as `m` and `ℓ` with `s = (tot − msub_new)/dt`, L369–371); it pushes through the pressure term `c_p²ℓ∇(w·msub)` (L447–451) and is advected by 0.3× the film velocity, not by any coffee flow. It is also switched off in `action_opt.py` defaults (A10). It does not supply a coffee velocity field or a liquid-milk volume.
3. *"The model does implement a coffee flow (`_bulk_flow`)."* True for `twolayer`/`v2` only (L766). The chi path never calls it; verified numerically (`Ub` attribute absent).
4. *"After the pour stops the structure is frozen, so nothing is missing."* The real question is what the model lacks; in the videos nothing dramatic happens after the lift either (video_obs L75: gaps form during the first ~1 s of each *new* pour, "not created by the lift or the cut"; L61: blob after lift is a soft-edged rounded blob). physics_prior L167/L178 ([I]-grade inference) expects 1–3 cm/s surface drift decaying over 1–3 s after the pour. So the absence of residual coffee circulation is real but its *importance* for the end pattern is not established by the evidence — the diagnosis itself only lists it as a missing ingredient.

## 4. Nuances (for `correction`)

1. **"膜被拖向静止" is exact; "结构一旦形成…被定在原地" is not literal.** After the inlet is switched off the film coasts: in the N = 64 test (#9 constants, 0.5 s low pour with u_in = 1.5 D/s) the white centroid moved from x = 0.071 to 0.029 (≈ 0.04 D) in the 0.5 s after the stop, |q|_max fell 0.85 → 0.08 in 0.5 s, KE fell by ~12× in 0.5 s (faster than the e^{−2βt} = 0.09 drag-only reference because pressure also redistributes it). So post-pour motion exists but is the film's own inertia (coasting distance ~u₀/β, cf. A4), not a coffee circulation.
2. **The missing "liquid part" is stronger than C22 states.** In family A the (1−χ) share of the milk is not "liquid milk without a lightening term"; it is **volume that disappears** (ledger closes on χ·S_eff only, `layer_error` = 0). At the low pour χ ≈ 1, so 100 % of 15 mL/s becomes white film and nothing builds the "base"; at the cut χ ≈ 0.04, so 96 % of the milk leaves the simulation. The pitcher side (POUR §1 账本) conserves V_cup exactly, the cup side discards it.
3. The claim should name its scope: it holds for `closure="chi"` (with or without the §14 patch). `twolayer`/`v2` do have a quasi-steady potential-flow coffee layer (`_bulk_flow`), though with no inertia/memory (code_map item 9) — that is claim-B territory.

## 5. Corrected statement

A8 (model A, `closure="chi"`): the film is the only dynamical object. Drag is −βq relative to the cup (β = 2.4 /s), no coffee velocity field exists on this code path (`_bulk_flow` is gated to twolayer/v2, L766; the κ_r "return flow" is a footprint-local target velocity), the (1−χ)-share of the poured milk is discarded (ledger closes on χ·S_eff·t; 96 % lost at the cut, 0 % at the low pour where everything becomes white), and once the inlet is off the only motion is the film's own inertia decaying at rate β (≈0.04 D of coasting in the test), not a residual coffee circulation. Evidence POUR L19 and conv [539] are correct; docs_map C22 is about family B and is a weak citation here.
