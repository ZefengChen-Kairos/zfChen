**Claim**: 杯几何固定：无杯倾（6/6 片段 15–25°）、无液面上升（150 mL 升 3 cm）、自由滑移壁；杯壁褐环只靠"白没铺到"。

## 1. What the code does

### 1.1 Cup-surface geometry (solver side, shared by all closures)
- `solver.Grid.__init__` (L111–131): square grid on [−½, ½]², `self.mask = self.r <= R` with `R = Numerics.cup_radius = 0.49` (L79); faces exist only between two inside cells (`fx`, `fy`, L120–121). Attributes of a built Grid: `N, h, x, y, r, mask, fx, fy, area, n_cells, zero, one, fx_h, fy_h, nfx, nfy` — **no tilt, slope, depth map, level or time dependence**.
- `Solver.__init__` (L228–231) builds the Grid unconditionally, before any closure branch → the chi path (model A) and v2 use the same domain and the same face masks. `Params` has 30 fields (`cp, beta, nu, D, kappa_Q, kappa_c, kappa_t, kappa_r, B_dep, p_dep, return_law, closure, g_red, Fr_c2, phi_foam, m_opaque, ent_coef, cell_frac, tau_d, sub_advect, sub_pressure, H, H_f, D_L, m_o, l_skin, tau_y, tau_y_crema, yield_eps, nu_max`): none is a wall, slip, meniscus, tilt or level parameter.
- Cells outside the disc are reset to ℓ = 1, q = 0, m = 0 every sub-step (L514–517); the domain never changes.

### 1.2 Cup tilt (pitcher side) — exists, but only as a collision obstacle
- `pitcher.Cup` (L190–198): `r_in 0.040, r_out 0.043, height 0.07, rim_z 0.010, tilt = radians(20.0)`. Docstring: "Cup as an obstacle: **coffee surface at world z = 0** (centre at the origin), open cylinder tilted by `tilt` towards the pitcher body".
- The only consumer of `cup.tilt` is `Pitcher.required_lift` (L367–402): a bisection on a vertical lift that keeps 1440 outer-wall samples of the pitcher out of the tilted cup. `Barista.step` (L724–727) applies that lift to `pose.tip[2]`. The tilt is a constant dataclass default: `Barista.__init__` L626 `self.cup = Cup() if cup is None else cup`; `action_opt.run_one` (L75) never passes `cup=`; nothing in `action_opt.py`, `optimize.py`, `cell_test.py`, `actions.py`, `v2_actions.py` writes `cup.tilt`.
- The jet always lands on the horizontal plane: `Pitcher.jet(..., z_surface=0.0)` L294, `z0 = origin[2] - z_surface` L306, `t_hit = (v0z + sqrt(v0z² + 2 g z0))/g` L310, `x_hit = origin[:2] + v0[:2] t_hit` L311; `Pitcher.step(pose, dt, z_surface=0.0)` L447 → L476. `grep z_surface` over the package: no caller ever passes a non-zero value. PITCHER_MODEL.md L65 states the same ("落到 z = 0 的时间 t*"); PITCHER_MODEL_V12_PLAN.md §16 (L293–295) lists "世界坐标自由液面，而非固定 z=0" and "杯体倾斜：…杯口椭圆 ≠ 实际液面" as **planned, not done** (docs_map P5).
- Pitcher-only replay of the six `scaled_moves` scripts (NX geometry, V0 = 250 mL, 1/120 s): `cup.tilt` stays 20° from first to last frame in all six; the collision lift is 0.77 / 0.85 / 0.90 cm in 57/1071, 19/851, 11/1721 frames (heart, push_heart, swan) and **0 frames** in layered_heart, tulip, leaf. So the tilt touches the surface pattern only through a ≤ 0.9 cm extra spout height during ≤ 5 % of frames, in three of six patterns.
- Net: the *video* cup tilt (15–25° at the start, righted within 1–2.5 s, video_obs §0) has no counterpart: in the model the liquid surface is a horizontal, concentric, never-moving disc; the pitcher-side tilt is constant and points the other way in time (present throughout, never righted).

### 1.3 Liquid level rise — absent on both sides
- Pitcher: `z_surface` fixed at 0 (above) although the ledger tracks `V_cup` exactly (L501, PitcherState L210). The scripted spout height `Move.z0/z1` is therefore a height above a fixed plane, while a real barista lowers the pitcher as the level rises (the videos show 1–2 cm spout clearance throughout); the model's fall height does not shrink as the cup fills.
- Solver v2: `H = 0.05` m constant (`Params` L62), `S_v = S_eff D_L / H` (L304); LATTE_MODEL.md L246: "H 咖啡深度 4–6 cm 直接量（**随液面上升会变，先取常数**）" (self-admission, docs_map L88). The poured volume is turned into a uniform surface sink −S_v/A_cup via `div − div.mean()` (L610; code_map item 8), i.e. v2 represents the added volume as surface convergence, not as a level rise.
- Solver chi (model A): there is no H at all; the whole received volume goes into film thickness ℓ through `s = χ S_eff K` (L333, L459–468); the dimensionless reference depth is D_L/k_Q ≈ 6 cm (LATTE L292). Cup area, domain and jet fall height are untouched.
- Magnitude: the "150 mL → 3 cm" figure (docs_map L221: π·4² cm² = 50.3 cm²) is an **upper bound**. Actual pours of the optimised scripts, from the result JSONs (`runs/action_opt/*/…json`, `V_end` with V0 = 250 mL): 47.5–130 mL over all runs; model-A best set `cf39`: 88–114 mL (→ 1.7–2.3 cm); my pitcher-only replay of the un-scaled scripts: heart 70.7 mL (1.41 cm), push_heart 57.8 (1.15), layered 71.2 (1.42), tulip 93.1 (1.85), leaf 92.1 (1.83), swan 91.0 (1.81 cm). So the realised rise is **1–2.3 cm**, comparable to the 1.5 cm low-pour spout height.

### 1.4 Wall condition — free slip, no penetration, no wall drag, in every closure
- No penetration: face masks `fx, fy` close every face touching an outside cell (Grid L120–121); `kernels.rusanov_fluxes` writes `0.0` on closed faces (L63–69, `else: Fx_qx = Fx_qy = 0`); `mass_fluxes`, `limited_face_*`, `finish_B` all take `fx, fy` (solver L454, L467, L477–478).
- Zero tangential stress on the chi path: viscosity is `g.diffusion(kvx, kvy, u)` (L537–538 explicit, L546 implicit) → `kernels.apply_diffusion` (L38–54) adds a face term **only if the face is active** → homogeneous Neumann on each velocity component. Numeric check: `diffusion(uniform field) = 0` exactly; `diffusion(x)` is non-zero only at wall cells (= 1/h one-sided residual), i.e. no Dirichlet (no-slip) term of order −2u/h². The only momentum sink is the basal drag −βq (A8), uniform in space, not a wall layer.
- Zero tangential stress on the v2 SSA path: `_ssa_operator` (L629–677) builds G with u_x rows on active x-faces, v_y rows on active y-faces, divergence rows on inside cells with the one-sided face gradient (`cell_deriv`, `cnt`-normalised), and shear rows **only at corners whose 2×2 cells are all inside** (`cm`, L641). Numeric check (N = 48, v2 Params): max|G z| = 0 for a rigid translation, 7.1e-15 for a rigid rotation (u = −y, v = x), 1.0 for a simple shear (sanity). The comment at L525 says it: "rigid motions free". LATTE_MODEL.md L159 ("q: 无穿透；面内应力为零（自由滑移）；关闭面不计入 Rusanov 通量和粘性泛函") and L166 ("杯壁自由滑移是简化：真实的弯月面会把奶泡粘住一些") describe exactly this, and because Grid/kernels are shared the same table holds for model A (the SSA row is v2-only, the Rusanov/diffusion rows are common).
- Brown annulus: there is no term that removes or pins m near r = 0.49 (no wall sink, no meniscus, no extra drag within a boundary band; `Params` has none). Whatever white reaches the wall stays there. So the 0.10–0.25 D brown ring of the videos (V3, 6/6) can appear in a simulation only because the transport did not carry white that far — the claim's "只靠白没铺到" is literally what the code allows.

## 3. Attempts to refute
1. *"The code does model a tilted cup (Cup.tilt = 20°)."* True on the pitcher side, but it is a collision obstacle only (§1.2): constant 20° for the whole script, never fed to the hit point, depth, or solver domain; it changes the spout height by ≤ 0.9 cm in ≤ 5 % of frames and in three patterns only. The claim is about the cup-surface geometry seen by the solver; there it is absent. The wording "无杯倾" should be narrowed, not withdrawn.
2. *"Level rise is modelled implicitly: ℓ grows (A) / the uniform sink (v2) accounts for the volume."* The volume is conserved on the pitcher side (`V_cup`) and enters the film, but no geometric consequence of a rising level exists: the jet fall height, the domain, the depth H and the cup area never change. v2's uniform sink is the *wrong* consequence (surface convergence everywhere, code_map item 8). So the refutation fails; it only shifts the item from "ignored" to "mis-represented" on the v2 side.
3. *"Free slip is physically fine for a foam on coffee — the wall only touches a meniscus."* Possibly, but that is a physics judgement, not a refutation of what the code does; LATTE L166 itself concedes the meniscus "会把奶泡粘住一些" and physics_prior §1.3 / N9 expect the raft not to reach the wall. The claim does not assert how much it matters.
4. *"The brown annulus could come from a mechanism in the code (e.g. the χ(Fr) switch or the entrainment cell)."* Those act near the jet, not near the wall; nothing in `Params` or `substep` is wall-local. The annulus in a simulation is purely "white did not get there".

## 4. Nuances (for `correction`)
1. **Scope of "无杯倾"**: the solver domain and the hit-point plane have no tilt; the pitcher module has a *fixed* 20° `Cup.tilt` used only for collision lift (never righted, unlike the video). Both facts should be stated.
2. **"150 mL 升 3 cm" is an upper bound**; the optimised scripts pour 48–130 mL (typically 55–105 mL), i.e. a 1–2.3 cm rise in the r = 4 cm cup, which is comparable to the 1.5 cm low-pour spout height, so the un-modelled drift is of the fall height (pitcher side) rather than of the pattern (in a vertical-walled cup a level rise does not deform the surface pattern; in a tapered cup a 2 cm rise dilates it by a few %).
3. **Importance is open** ([minor] tag is right): the 20° tilt during the first 1–2.5 s makes the domain an ellipse with axis ratio 1/cos 20° = 1.064 and gives a depth difference 2r·tan 20° ≈ 2.9 cm between the B and D sides exactly during the "no white yet" window; whether that changes the first ring or the push-heart gaps is not shown by any evidence here. Experiment that would settle it: pour a heart with the cup level from t = 0 vs tilted-then-righted, same pitcher path, and compare first-white time and the initial C-ring shape.
